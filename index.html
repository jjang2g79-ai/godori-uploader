Godori Uploader

Godori Uploader is a small personal desktop tool made by the creator of the cat short-video series Godori AI Lab (고돌이네 AI연구소).

When a new cat short video is finished, the tool adds it to a local "Post to TikTok" page on the creator's own computer. On that page the creator signs in with TikTok, previews the video, edits the caption, chooses who can view it and which interactions to allow, and presses Post. Nothing is posted without the creator pressing Post.

What it uses

  • Login Kit (user.info.basic) — shows the connected account's nickname and avatar on the posting page.
  • Content Posting API (video.upload, video.publish) — sends a video as a draft, or posts it after the creator confirms it.

The tool is used only with the creator's own TikTok account and only for videos the creator made. It is not offered to other users.
